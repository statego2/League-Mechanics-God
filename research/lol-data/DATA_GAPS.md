# Gaps and conflicts

- Current full ability configurations for Ahri and Tristana are **not complete**. Many windups, ratios, E/W/R details, collision radii, cooldown/rank scaling and minion projectile timing need current sourced verification. Historical Meraki content includes richer candidates but is stale and unparsed. Tristana range interpolation must be verified: the current wiki endpoint is 717.65, whereas old Meraki records show 700; don't linearly extrapolate the old range.
- Riot 26.1 describes siege base AD 39 and super base AD 180; current wiki summaries display 37.5 and 185 respectively. Resolve conditions, wave upgrades, or subsequent patch changes before setting either. `null` reflects the unresolved conflict. Full growth functions and exact minion base damage, windups, projectiles, armor, collision and turret timings remain incomplete.
- Minion AI priority and call-for-help are source-reported, not experimentally reproduced. First-wave special behavior, target reevaluation cadence and turret targeting require controlled observations. Practice Tool has a reported wave interval bug; don't assume its schedule matches normal SR.
- 26.1 specifies interval thresholds, but boundary waves should be tested against the explicitly recorded examples. Siege spawning when inhibitors change and stage transition edge cases require verification. XP and CS need per-queue, team, range, kill credit, and environmental-death tests. Gold and XP are distinct from last-hit scoring.
- Movement acceleration, tick timing, pathing, obstacle avoidance, attack-move cursor settings and buffering, negative-resist damage, attack-speed cap, special windup modifiers, and simultaneous death ordering are unverified here. Projectile width is full width, not radius. Terminal checks, targeted missiles, split and returning paths require individual rules.
- The schema and tests check representation, source consistency and chosen examples; they do not validate in-client fidelity. Wiki observations carry retrieval date and target patch rather than immutable patch evidence. `riot_stat_units` preserves native source units where no reliable conversion has been made.
