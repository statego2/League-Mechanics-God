# Sources and provenance

- [Riot version list](https://ddragon.leagueoflegends.com/api/versions.json), [pinned champion roster](https://ddragon.leagueoflegends.com/cdn/16.21.1/data/en_US/champion.json), and [Data Dragon documentation](https://developer.riotgames.com/docs/lol): official static data, retrieved 2026-10-30. Each normalized roster stat preserves exact JSON path. Stats are nominal values, not a complete runtime formula or guarantee of in-game accuracy.
- [Riot 26.1](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/patch-26-1-notes/) establishes 2026 minion schedules and economy. [Riot 26.21](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-21-notes/) verifies Tristana Q rank values. [Riot 13.22](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-gb/news/game-updates/patch-13-22-notes/) documents Ahri's attack-speed ratio and windup changes; confirm no later change before treating as final.
- [League Wiki minions](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Minions), [Ahri](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Ahri), [Tristana](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Tristana), [Blitzcrank](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Blitzcrank), [Vel'Koz](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Vel%27Koz), and individual minion pages document behaviors not supplied by Data Dragon. Direct web access was blocked by robots; search-index excerpts were inspectable. This limits confidence and coverage; inspect full articles or validate in-client before building detailed simulation. Historical wiki farming guidance contains obsolete first-wave timing.
- [Meraki source and documentation](https://github.com/meraki-analytics/lolstaticdata) identify its data as sourced partly from community wiki and CommunityDragon. Its latest CDN yielded 171 champions and 15.15.1 asset URLs, versus 173 from Riot. It is stale; observed data should not be automatically called 16.21. It uses complex source strings, not a numerically normalized ability schema. Credit Meraki and League Wiki if using its material.
- [CommunityDragon character directory](https://raw.communitydragon.org/latest/game/data/characters/) and [documentation](https://github.com/CommunityDragon/Docs): raw game files exist for champions and other game entities, but are technically complex and `latest` is mutable. A requested pinned 16.21 champion-summary endpoint returned 404; no raw values were interpreted as verified. Do not assume a requested raw field represents live runtime behavior without interpretation and patch validation.

Riot's [developer policy](https://developer.riotgames.com/docs/lol) currently says not to create games using Riot IP or closely resembling its games. This is material to the stated product and warrants review before integrating or distributing Riot-specific data. Research inclusion here is not a licensing determination.
