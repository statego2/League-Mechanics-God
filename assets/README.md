# Original generated art

These assets were generated for this project with the built-in image generation tool, independently of the League of Dodge files.

- `arena-lane.png` — prompt: "Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: playable browser MOBA arena ground background. Create an original polished hand-painted fantasy game terrain, viewed from an elevated three-quarter top-down camera, wide landscape 16:9. A broad weathered stone and earth lane crossing diagonally southwest to northeast, surrounded by rich dark green grass, moss, scattered small rocks and low foliage, subtle ambient light and readable open central combat area. Ground plane only, no characters, no UI, no text, no logos, no structures or impassable objects. Cohesive detailed game texture suitable beneath small player sprites."
- `ranger.png` — prompt: "Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: isolated game character sprite. Original fantasy ranger champion, full body in dynamic ready stance, seen from elevated three-quarter camera for a top-down MOBA game. Teal hood and armor, cream leather accents, small bow, readable heroic silhouette and boots visible, polished hand-painted game art, lit from upper left. Single character only, centered, full body with small margin, transparent background, no ground, no shadow, no text, no logo, no borders."

The background is illustrative. Two collision proxies are now drawn over stone-wall areas; other rocks and vegetation are decorative. The ranger is one static pose with flip, bob and windup movement, not a full directional animation set. Both have procedural fallback rendering if the images fail to load.

Center clear for combat, consistent scale, painted 3D game art, natural sunlight, no characters, no UI, no words, no logos, no towers or solid objects in the playable lane."
- `mage.png` — prompt: "Original enemy mage character sprite, single full-body armored red and dark crimson battle mage with staff, viewed from elevated three-quarter camera for a top-down fantasy MOBA, polished game art, obvious hostile red accents, distinct silhouette, full character centered with margin, transparent background, no text, no ground, no extra characters."
- `minion.png` — prompt: "Original small fantasy MOBA enemy melee minion, single full-body red and bronze armored humanoid minion with helmet and short sword, three-quarter overhead perspective, clearly visible broad shape, polished stylized game sprite, centered with margin, transparent background, no text, no ground, no other characters."

The new background evokes mid lane but is not an exact recreation of its layout. Two marked wall proxies have collision; most river, walls and foliage remain decorative. Mage and minion are single poses; allied minions use a hue-shifted version. Character drawing and original procedural terrain remain available as fallbacks.

The ranger has three directional illustrations with left/right mirroring and procedural bob/tilt. They are static illustrations, not frame-by-frame animation.
